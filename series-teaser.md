## Medium Version (Expanded)

### The SDLC is Dead. Here's What Replaces It.

You've spent decades optimising it. Sprints. Story points. Planning poker. Code reviews. Release trains. CAB meetings.

It all assumes one thing: humans are the bottleneck.

Agents broke that assumption. Now your SDLC is drowning in its own ceremonies.

When an AI agent generates a working feature in two hours, your sprint planning becomes absurd. When agents ship code faster than humans can review it, your PR process becomes theatre. When compliance can be baked into agent logic, your approval gates become expensive ceremony.

The uncomfortable reality: **the SDLC you've optimised for years isn't slow. It's dead.**

Not because it's bad. Because it was built for human speed, and agents operate at machine speed. And you can't just run the same process faster. You have to replace it entirely.

---

### What We're Exploring

Over the next three weeks, we're diving into three uncomfortable truths:

**Part 1: Operating the SDLC in the Dark**

The dark factory isn't science fiction—it's the inevitable destination. When code commits trigger autonomous verification, releases flow continuously, incidents self-heal, and compliance is enforced by policy instead of approval meetings, what does governance look like? What human roles survive? And how do you ensure safety when observability—not code review—becomes your last line of defence?

**Part 2: The Alignment Crisis**

Speed without alignment is chaos at scale. Agents can write code at inhuman velocity. But when one agent is refactoring auth, another is building payments, and a third is rewriting the API—all in parallel, all without coordination—you're building incompatible things at 10x speed. GitHub research teams put it plainly: *"We're at risk of burning through a billion tokens in exchange for code no one understands."*

Current tools (PRs, Slack, Jira) can't maintain alignment at agentic velocity. We explore why the bottleneck has shifted from writing code to **agreeing on what to build**.

**Part 3: Collaborative Agentic Sessions**

The solution isn't better PRs or faster reviews. It's a completely different model: synchronous, real-time, multi-role collaboration where planning and building happen in the same conversation, with everyone seeing the same thing at the same time.

This means rethinking roles, rewriting tooling, and accepting that the future of development looks nothing like what came before.
