Your SDLC Doesn't Need to Be Faster.It Needs to Be Dead.

When agents operate at machine speed, every assumption your SDLC was built on collapses.

The prevailing narrative is that AI is a "10x developer tool." That's wrong.

The agentic era doesn't make your current SDLC faster. It kills it.

When an agent generates 1000 pull requests per week, your team reviewing 10 per day isn't a bottleneck. It's a fake bottleneck. You're forcing a human ritual onto a machine workflow.

Ceremonies (sprints, story points, planning poker, CAB meetings, release trains) consume 30% of team capacity and agents eliminate the need for them entirely. You're not speeding up the SDLC. You're erasing it.

The shift isn't "faster humans." It's "humans governing agents." And that requires a completely new model.
