400 PRs OvernightWhy Code Review is Dead at Agentic Scale

The math doesn't work. It never will.

Here's a concrete scenario that every engineering leader should dread:

An agent refactors a deprecated library across your entire codebase and submits 400 PRs overnight.

Your team takes 10 minutes to review each PR. That's standard—you need to check logic, test coverage, security implications.

400 PRs × 10 mins/PR = 66 hours of manual review work

Your team can't actually execute this review. They have sprint commitments. They have other work.

So what do you do?

  • Option A: Rubber-stamp all 400 PRs. You're not actually reviewing anything.
  • Option B: Let them languish. They're already in production behind a feature flag.

Either way, code review as a control mechanism is dead.

This isn't a bottleneck problem. It's a paradigm collapse. The entire SDLC was engineered for human speed. When agents operate at machine velocity, every mechanism that kept it together—review, approval, pacing—fails.
